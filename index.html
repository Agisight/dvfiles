My DV Files

Diversity Visa entry organizer for iPhone and Android.

Keep your family's details, photos, documents and confirmation number on your own phone, read plain-language explanations next to the official instructions, and get reminders about season dates.

The app never submits anything for you and has no server: your data stays on your phone or in a place you choose.

This site

Hosts the Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use and a public season dates file the app reads. It contains no personal data.

My DV Files is an independent organizer app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the U.S. Department of State or any government agency. Entries are submitted only by the entrant on dvprogram.state.gov.
