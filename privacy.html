Privacy Policy — My DV Files

Draft for attorney review. Replace every [BRACKETED] value before publishing.

Last updated: [DATE]

[COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us") makes the My DV Files app (the "App"). This policy explains what happens to your information.

Short version

  • We do not collect, receive, or store the information you enter in the App.
  • Your data and documents stay on your phone, or in a place you choose: your iCloud Drive, a folder you pick, a file you save, or an app you share with.
  • Photos and document scans are taken, checked and edited on your phone (including face detection and background replacement). They are never uploaded by the App.
  • The App has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

1. Information you enter

Names, dates and places of birth, passport details, address, phone, email, education, family members' details, photos, your confirmation number, and copies of documents you add (for example passports, national IDs, birth/marriage/divorce/death certificates, diplomas, work records, and confirmation pages) are stored:

  • in the App's private storage on your device; and
  • only if you turn it on or choose it:
  • in an App folder in your iCloud Drive ("My DV Files"), synced by Apple to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account;
  • in a folder you pick on your device or in a storage app (Android);
  • in a linked file or a backup file you save to a location you select;
  • in any app you send a file to with the share sheet.

These locations are provided by Apple, Google or another provider and are governed by that provider's privacy policy, not ours. A backup file contains your entry and all your documents in one file — anyone who gets that file can read it.

We have no access to any of them. If you delete the App, its private storage is deleted by your device. Files and folders you saved elsewhere (including iCloud Drive) stay there until you delete them.

Camera and on-device processing

If you use "Take photo" or "Scan a document", the App uses your camera only while that screen is open. Live frames are analyzed on your device (face position, document edges) and discarded; only the photo or scan you keep is saved. Face detection, photo checks and background replacement run entirely on your device; no image is sent to us or to any third-party service by the App.

Passport scan (text recognition on the device)

If you use "Scan passport", the App reads the two machine-readable lines at the bottom of your passport's photo page with the text recognition built into your phone (Apple Vision on iPhone; Google ML Kit text recognition bundled in the App on Android). This runs entirely on your device. Camera frames and the photo you choose for scanning are analyzed in memory and discarded — they are not saved, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Only the values you check and save are stored, in the same place as the rest of your entry.

Voice reading

If you tap "Listen", the App reads the explanation text aloud with your phone's own system voice (text-to-speech). The text is the same for every user, and the App does not record audio or use the microphone. Whether speech is processed on the device depends on the voice your phone's system provides; the App itself sends nothing.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect usage analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, device identifiers, or the contents of your documents.

3. Network access

The App connects to the internet only to:

  • download a public file with the current season's dates and official links from agisight.github.io (GitHub Pages). This request contains no personal information. Like any web request, the hosting provider may see your IP address in its standard server logs; we do not receive or use it.
  • open official U.S. Government websites in your browser when you tap a link.
  • process purchases through Apple or Google.
  • (only if you turn it on) let your device's iCloud Drive or storage app sync the folder you chose; this syncing is done by Apple or that provider, not by us.
  • (Android, document scanning) the system document scanner is provided by Google Play services and runs on your device.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google. We receive only an anonymous confirmation that a purchase is valid. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details.

5. Children

The App is intended for adults preparing their own entry. Parents may enter information and photos of their children as required by the official instructions; this information stays on the parent's device or in a file the parent controls, and we do not receive it.

6. Security

Data in the App's private storage is protected by your device's security. Keep your device locked and be careful who you share your profile file, backup file or documents with, because they contain personal information and copies of identity documents.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal information, we cannot access, correct, export, or delete it for you. You can view, edit, export, and delete it yourself in the App and in the storage location you chose.

8. Changes

We will update this policy if the App's data practices change. If we ever begin collecting personal information, we will ask for your consent in the App first.
